# Privacy and limitations

## Privacy and security

- The verified server mode binds to loopback.
- No credential is required for the local or synthetic-fixture walkthrough.
- Provider credentials, private response binaries, local run stores, webcam recordings, weights, and third-party videos are excluded.
- Webcam capture must be user-initiated. Its future engineering increment requires separate independent acceptance before new claims are made.
- A future provider-enabled workflow would send data outside the machine and requires a new authorization and privacy review.

## Limitations

- Zonivra is not certified, safety-critical, or collision avoidance.
- No benchmark, accuracy, distance, collision-prediction, or production-performance claim is made.
- RF-DETR runs locally when weights are provided, but the industrial accuracy of this configuration is not validated.
- The RED drill is synthetic.
- Explanations are simulated and subordinate to deterministic records.
- The accepted Meta evidence is one synthetic image and one observed external response profile.
- Native live Meta inference remains disabled.
- SAM mask pixels are not rendered.
- macOS is required for the current MP4 decoder.
- Public reproduction of genuine Meta evidence is limited because the private provider response is not redistributed.
- Legal ownership, employer rights, competition eligibility, trademark, patent, and final third-party license clearance require owner/legal confirmation.
